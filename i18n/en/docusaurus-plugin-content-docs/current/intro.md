# Runify

**Lightweight White-box Multi-Agent Platform**

Low footprint · Visual building · Fully controllable custom AI Agents

> **No more black boxes and magic — everything under your control.** In Runify, every reasoning step, every tool call and every decision is fully transparent, editable and debuggable. You stay in charge of how your AI assistant behaves.

## What problem it solves

Most AI tools hide their reasoning inside a black box, making them hard to understand, correct and trust. Runify lays every decision out on a visual canvas, letting you compose an assistant like building blocks and step in, edit and re-run anything at any time.

## Feature overview

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **White-box** | Reasoning, tool calls and decisions are transparent, editable and debuggable |
| **Lightweight** | Typically starts in 12 seconds and uses 100MB–200MB of memory while running — an ordinary laptop is enough |
| **Visual building** | Drag-and-drop visual flow canvas, with no special diagram standard to learn |
| **Knowledge base** | Import PDF / DOC / DOCX / ZIP documents and find the right information with built-in full-text search |
| **Data sources** | Connect to the business databases you already use, with a simple setup in the interface |
| **Cross-platform** | Use Runify in a web browser or as a desktop app for macOS / Windows |

## Platform capabilities

Beyond the visual canvas, Runify ships a set of first-class capabilities:

- **Skills and a Skill store** — package reusable capabilities, then install and upgrade them from the store; assistants can call these from a workflow.
- **Third-party integrations** — connect Runify to the messaging and collaboration tools your team already uses.
- **Context management** — a separate way to keep long conversations on track: the assistant condenses earlier messages, pulls out important facts as notes and recalls them in later turns.
- **AI-generated workflows** — describe what you want in plain language and Runify drafts the flow for you; refine it on the canvas.
- **Publishing and versions** — publish your work and keep a history of versions you can return to.
- **Role-based permissions** — decide who can view, edit and publish each resource.
- **Embed into existing websites and systems** — place a Runify chat into your own website or internal system.
- **A dedicated chat page** — end users open a clean, standalone chat page to talk to a published assistant.

When you delete a knowledge base, its documents are removed too, so nothing is left behind.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants an AI assistant they can understand and control, without needing to be a programmer.
- Small teams that want to run an assistant on an everyday laptop or a modest office server, keeping their data in-house.
- Businesses that want the assistant to work with their own documents and business data.
- Teams that need approval steps, access control and a clear record of what the assistant did.
